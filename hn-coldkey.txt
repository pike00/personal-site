Show HN: Coldkey – Paper backup for post-quantum age encryption keys

I use age and SOPS to encrypt secrets in git. The weak link is the private key: lose it and everything encrypted to it is gone forever. Digital backups share the same failure domain as the original.

Coldkey generates post-quantum (ML-KEM-768 + X25519) age keys and produces a single-page printable HTML backup with QR codes. Print it, laminate it, put it in a fireproof safe. Recovery is scanning the QR codes back.

Key design decisions:
- The 32-byte seed fits in one version-40 QR code (~2KB). Larger keys auto-split with a framing protocol + SHA-256 checksum.
- mlockall prevents key material from swapping to disk
- Docker hardened mode: distroless/static:nonroot, --network none, --read-only, --cap-drop ALL
- Homebrew tap, Go install, or ghcr.io container

I wrote it because the standard advice is "back up your keys" but nobody specifies how in a way that survives a house fire. A paper backup breaks the trust chain: the key lives in a different physical domain from the secrets it protects.
